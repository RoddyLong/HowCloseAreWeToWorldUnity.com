THE MODEL

Core Philosophy & Objective Matrix

What this measure can—and cannot—tell us about cooperation at planetary scale.

01

What is this counter?

World unity is a continuing journey toward global cooperation, collective action, and shared human prosperity—not a single centralized state. This index tracks a narrow stream of public reports that match cooperation criteria; it is not a census of humanity or a direct measure of how united people feel.

02

Is absolute world unity genuinely possible?

Political divisions and conflict are real. Yet people already cooperate across borders through shared science, public health, trade, communications, and humanitarian response. These are meaningful forms of functional unity, without implying that disagreement or inequality has disappeared.

03

How to contribute

  • Support reputable international charities and local groups serving diverse communities.
  • Build cross-cultural understanding through listening and communication.
  • Join open research or citizen-science projects that share knowledge globally.
Scoring, sources and limitations

The starting index is a project-set 8.4200%, not a measured historical estimate. Qualifying reports add volume units (1 for community/public good, 10 for institutional cooperation, 100 for qualifying treaties). Every 1,000 units, rounded-down cumulative volume is used in the logarithmic curve: 0.0100% × log₂(1 + rounded volume / 100,000). At 100,000 community report units, the cumulative change is +0.0100%. Reports are keyword-filtered, not independently verified acts.

Google News RSS is queried for AP and Reuters coverage; it is an aggregator, not a direct wire feed. Community items come from Reddit. Updates are checked every 30 seconds, but upstream publication and provider delays mean this is not a second-by-second global monitor. Source URLs are supplied by feeds and are not independently authenticated by this project.
