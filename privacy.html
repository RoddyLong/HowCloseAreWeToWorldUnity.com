Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Information We Collect

This website does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. We do not use analytics, tracking cookies, or third-party tracking services.

Local Storage Only: We use your browser's localStorage to remember which news headlines you have already seen. This data stays on your device only and is never sent to our servers or any third party. You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser's local storage or using your browser's privacy settings.

Cookies and Third-Party Services

This site does not use cookies for tracking purposes. However, third-party ad networks may place cookies on your device as part of displaying advertisements. These cookies are controlled by the ad networks, not by us. Please refer to the respective ad network's privacy policy for information on their cookie practices.

We use the following third-party services:

  • Ad Networks: For displaying advertisements (see their privacy policies for details)
  • CDN: unpkg.com for loading the Globe.gl library (no tracking)
  • RSS Feeds: Public RSS feeds from news organizations (no personal data exchanged)

Data Retention

Local storage data is automatically cleared if it exceeds 500 items. You can manually clear it at any time through your browser settings. Since this data never leaves your device, we have no record of it and cannot access it.

Your Rights

Under GDPR and other privacy regulations, you have the right to:

  • Access data stored in your browser's localStorage
  • Delete this data at any time
  • Opt out of personalized advertising through your browser settings

Since we don't collect personal data, we have no data to delete from our servers.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Since we do not collect personal data, this is not applicable to our service.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by posting the new policy on this page.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through our contact page.
